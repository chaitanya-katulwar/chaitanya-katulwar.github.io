Chaitanya Katulwar

// User Experience Designer

Hi, I’m Chaitanya — a designer focused on interaction craft, scalable design systems, and bridging the gap between design vision and production engineering.

As the Interaction Designer at bookmyshow, my day to day involves building and maintaining our multi-platform design system across Android, iOS, and Web, designing core consumer booking funnels, and defining motion physics and micro-interactions for millions of users.

Previously at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), I led the end-to-end UX for an airport hospitality operations platform — designing a desktop operations portal for supervisors and a tablet application for ground agents to streamline real-time services from VIP meet-and-greet to lounge access.

When I’m not refining design architectures or prototyping interactions, you’ll usually find me listening to Rock Music, watching movies, and playing story based video games.
